Data & methodology
Source
Trendlyne. Exports refresh monthly; an export date is not a guarantee of fresh source data for every index.
Returns
Calendar-year and quarterly index returns. The current year's annual value is YTD, not a completed year. Blank values mean unavailable or unfinished periods.
Growth of ₹10,000
Quarterly compounding on the latest uninterrupted history shared by all selected indices. Each line starts at ₹10,000 immediately before the first included quarter. Only completed quarters are included; the result can differ from the latest annual YTD value. Changing the selection or range resets the common starting date.
Assumptions
Hypothetical index growth, not an investable portfolio. No fees, taxes or additional cash flows; dividends are not added separately.
CAGR
Stat tiles use the last 4 × N available consecutive quarterly returns. Historical table values compound N calendar years; current-year CAGR ends at the last available completed quarter.
Statistics and filters
Best/worst year, up years and annual volatility use completed years across the full available history. Stat tiles are not changed by the chart/table year filters. The Summary view always uses all available data.
Risk
Annual volatility is sample standard deviation. The summary's risk-adjusted score is mean annual return divided by annual volatility, not a Sharpe ratio. Past performance does not predict future returns; this is not investment advice.
